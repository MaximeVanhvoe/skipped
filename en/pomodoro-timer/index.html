Questions

Frequently asked.

Is there a free pomodoro app that blocks apps?

Yes. Skipped's study mode is free and blocks distracting apps with Screen Time on iPhone and iPad.

Do blocked apps come back on their own?

Yes, at your break and at the end of your session, even when Skipped is closed.

Can I set the length of a pomodoro?

Yes. Choose pomodoro, a fixed time or free, and adjust your break and extra time.
