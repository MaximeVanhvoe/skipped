Questions

Frequently asked.

What is Skipped?

Skipped: Class Tracker is a free app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that keeps track of which classes you attended and which you missed. Your schedule comes from Apple Calendar, and everything you still need to catch up on is in To Do.

How do I keep track of the classes I missed?

Mark each class in Skipped as attended or missed. A missed class gets a red pill and goes to To Do, sorted by course, until you have caught up.

Does Skipped work with my university or college timetable?

Yes, as long as your timetable is in Apple Calendar, for example through a calendar subscription (iCal link) from your school. Skipped reads your classes from there, so there is nothing to type over.

Is Skipped free?

Yes. Skipped is free for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and you don't need to create an account. Your data syncs to your other devices through iCloud.

Can I study with a timer and block distracting apps?

Yes. In study mode you choose free, pomodoro or a fixed time. The timer shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and on iPhone and iPad you can block apps through Screen Time.

Can I study together with friends?

Yes. See who is studying right now and how many hours your friends put in, and start a group for exam season. You choose what you share with each friend and each group.

Do I need an iPhone?

The app is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. On any other computer you can open your schedule with Skipped on the web.
